# 1. Overview

## Project Concept

A centralized platform for an organization operating multiple sports academies, gyms and
fitness facilities. It manages the athlete journey from registration and academy assignment
through training, attendance, performance, practice levels, physician sessions, competitions
and transfers.

## Portfolio Positioning

> A Multi-Academy Sports, Fitness & Athlete Development Management Platform that provides a
> continuous athlete profile across multiple academies, training, performance, medical support,
> competitions and transfers.

The key differentiator is **continuity**: an athlete's history (attendance, assessments,
practice level, medical clearance, competitions, achievements) follows them across academies
and programs rather than resetting or fragmenting on transfer.
